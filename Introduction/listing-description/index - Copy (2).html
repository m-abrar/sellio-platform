The foundation

Start with marketplace work that already connects.

Sellio brings the recurring parts of a marketplace into one codebase so your effort can move toward branding, business rules, content, and launch preparation.

A
Central administration

Users, roles, modules, listings, payments, subscriptions, withdrawals, content, settings, and reporting.

S
Seller operations

Vertical-aware listings, orders, bookings, inquiries, analytics, memberships, wallet activity, and payouts.

B
Buyer workspace

Favorites, messages, notifications, orders, bookings, applications, inquiries, reviews, and profile tools.

M
Mobile customer journey

Expo/React Native discovery, account activity, messaging, push notifications, and secure checkout handoff.

Three connected perspectives

Each person sees the tools they need.

The same marketplace data moves through dedicated administration, seller, and buyer experiences.

Administrator
  • Configure modules and settings
  • Moderate users and listings
  • Manage payments and subscriptions
  • Review reports and activity
Seller
  • Create vertical-aware listings
  • Handle orders and customer actions
  • Track inventory and performance
  • Manage wallet and withdrawals
Buyer
  • Discover and save listings
  • Chat with marketplace sellers
  • Buy, book, apply, or inquire
  • Track account activity
