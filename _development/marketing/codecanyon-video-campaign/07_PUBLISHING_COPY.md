## Short Description

Sellio is a self-hosted multi-vendor marketplace platform for ecommerce, properties, vehicles, events, jobs, services, and classifieds. Manage storefronts, sellers, buyers, listings, bookings, payments, subscriptions, messaging, and marketplace operations from one connected system.

Explore the live demos and get Sellio on CodeCanyon.

## Long Description

Launch and manage multiple marketplace business models with Sellio, a modular self-hosted platform built with Laravel, Next.js, and React.

Sellio combines:

- Ecommerce products and orders
- Property sales and rental bookings
- Vehicle listings and inquiries
- Events and ticket bookings
- Jobs and applications
- Services, appointments, and quotes
- Classified listings and customer inquiries
- Unified multi-vertical storefront experiences
- Dedicated seller and buyer portals
- Administration, moderation, subscriptions, payments, content, and reporting

Use the live demos to explore the public storefronts and the dedicated admin, seller, and buyer experiences.
