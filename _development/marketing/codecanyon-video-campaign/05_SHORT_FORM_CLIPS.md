# Short-Form Video Plan

## Format

- Length: 20-40 seconds
- Master: 1920x1080
- Social export: 1080x1920 with captions inside safe areas
- Structure: hook, proof, outcome, call to action
- Each clip must make sense without audio.

## Vertical Clips

### Ecommerce

**Hook:** `Launch a modern multi-vendor store.`

Show storefront, product detail, cart, checkout, seller product management, and admin orders.

### Properties

**Hook:** `Build property sales and rental marketplaces.`

Show search, map/date filtering, property detail, rental booking, seller property form, and admin bookings.

### Vehicles

**Hook:** `Create a vehicle marketplace for dealers and buyers.`

Show vehicle search, detail/gallery, inquiry, seller listing management, and admin inquiries.

### Events

**Hook:** `Publish events and sell bookable tickets.`

Show event discovery, ticket selection, checkout, confirmation, seller management, and admin bookings.

### Jobs

**Hook:** `Launch a job board with connected applications.`

Show job search, job detail, application flow, buyer application tracking, seller job management, and admin moderation.

### Services

**Hook:** `Connect customers with service providers.`

Show service discovery, detail, quote/appointment flow, buyer activity, seller requests, and admin operations.

### Classifieds

**Hook:** `Power a local or niche classifieds marketplace.`

Show browse/search, listing detail, seller contact or inquiry, seller ad management, and admin moderation.

### Unified Marketplace

**Hook:** `Bring every marketplace vertical together.`

Show the unified home, cross-vertical categories, explore results, detail routes, and administration dashboard.

## Feature Clips

### Storefront Themes

Rapidly transition through 8-12 of the strongest polished themes. Use the caption:

`Purpose-built storefronts for different marketplace businesses.`

### Seller Portal

Show dashboard, listing creation, Media Studio, messages, wallet, and subscriptions.

### Buyer Portal

Show favorites, bookings, applications, inquiries, messages, and reviews.

### Marketplace Administration

Show dashboard, moderation, modules, plans, payments, content, and reports.

### Installation and Ownership

Show documentation, installer, completed setup, and storefront. Use only verified statements:

`Self-hosted • Full source code • Built with Laravel, Next.js, and React`

## Reusable Ending

- Sellio logo.
- `Explore the live demos.`
- `Available on CodeCanyon.`
- Marketing or item URL added during final publishing.
