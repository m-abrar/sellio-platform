# Seller Journey Video

## Goal

Show prospective buyers that partners receive a genuine operating portal capable of supporting several marketplace business models.

## Target Length

3-4 minutes.

## Storyboard

### 1. Seller dashboard — 00:00-00:30

- Open the populated dashboard.
- Highlight listing counts, activity, revenue/wallet information, and alerts.
- Keep the opening energetic; do not begin by typing credentials.

### 2. Manage verticals — 00:30-01:05

Use quick cuts across products, properties, autos, events, jobs, services, and classifieds.

**Narration point:** A seller can operate different listing businesses from one consistent workspace while each vertical retains its own fields and workflows.

### 3. Create a listing — 01:05-02:05

Use the most visually compelling stable form—property or product is preferred.

- Choose category/type.
- Complete representative core fields.
- Show pricing and vertical-specific options.
- Upload or select media in Media Studio.
- Reorder gallery items and select a primary image.
- Show coordinates/map pin only when the configured map works reliably.
- Save and show deterministic success feedback.
- Open the resulting listing or edit screen.

The final edit may compress form entry with speed ramps, but the save result must remain visible long enough to feel credible.

### 4. Customer operations — 02:05-02:50

- Open messages.
- Show leads, bookings, inquiries, applications, or service requests.
- Show one status change or response where safe.

### 5. Business and account management — 02:50-03:25

- Subscription/plan usage.
- Wallet and withdrawal surface.
- Settings and storefront/business identity.
- Briefly show notification feedback.

### 6. Close — 03:25-03:40

**Voice-over**

> Sellio gives every partner the tools to publish, communicate, and operate—without exposing the complexity of the administration system.

**Call to action**

`Explore the seller demo • Available on CodeCanyon`

## Capture Requirements

- Use the seeded partner account with an active plan and enough quota.
- Prepare one draft listing to complete during recording.
- Verify create, edit, media, and navigation behavior immediately before capture.
- Populate messages, wallet activity, and at least two lead/request types.
- Clear browser developer tools and internal setup-warning banners.
- Never show API keys, environment configuration, or real payout details.
