## Editing Rules

- Start with the outcome, not the login screen.
- Never leave the cursor idle or show loading states longer than half a second.
- Use subtle zooms and highlights to direct attention.
- Show authentic populated data; avoid empty tables and zero-value dashboards.
- Keep browser chrome consistent or crop it out.
- Do not display secrets, private keys, local paths, debug toolbars, or personal notifications.
- Avoid unsupported superlatives such as “unlimited,” “enterprise-grade,” or “handles millions” unless tested and documented.
