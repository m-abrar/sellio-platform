# Sellio CodeCanyon Video Campaign

## Objective

Create a coordinated set of sales videos that explains Sellio quickly, proves that its major workflows are real, and directs prospective buyers to the live demos and CodeCanyon item page.

Sellio should be positioned as a self-hosted, modular marketplace platform rather than as a single-purpose ecommerce script. The strongest verified product story is:

- Laravel administration and public Blade storefront
- Next.js storefront with multiple vertical-specific themes
- Dedicated React seller and buyer portals
- Products, properties, vehicles, events, jobs, services, and classifieds
- A unified marketplace experience across those verticals
- Payments, bookings, inquiries, applications, messaging, subscriptions, commissions, and reporting
- Full source-code ownership without a recurring SaaS platform fee

Do not claim a feature merely because it appears in marketing copy. Every recorded claim must be demonstrated on the production demo or confirmed in the current release candidate.

## Campaign Deliverables

| Deliverable | Target length | Primary purpose |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| Master sales trailer | 75-90 seconds | Fast value proposition and CodeCanyon conversion |
| Buyer journey | 3-4 minutes | Demonstrate discovery, checkout/booking, and account activity |
| Seller journey | 3-4 minutes | Demonstrate listing creation and business operations |
| Admin tour | 4-6 minutes | Establish depth, control, and buyer confidence |
| Feature shorts | 20-40 seconds each | Social posts, listing embeds, and modular promotion |

## Recommended Production Order

1. Stabilize and reseed all public demos.
2. Capture clean reusable footage for every portal.
3. Produce the master trailer first.
4. Reuse the footage for the three long-form tours.
5. Cut vertical and feature shorts from the same source footage.
6. Publish the trailer only after every visible URL and workflow is stable.
