# Sellio Test Voice-Over

## Purpose

Use this short script to test the narrator, microphone, pacing, music level, and pronunciation before recording the full Sellio video campaign.

- Target length: 45-55 seconds
- Tone: confident, warm, clear, and conversational
- Pace: approximately 135-145 words per minute
- Pronunciation: **Sellio** = **SELL-ee-oh**
- Delivery: pause briefly at each em dash; give the final line a little more energy

## Test Notes

After recording, check that:

- “Sellio,” “Laravel,” and “Next.js” sound natural and consistent.
- Every word remains clear under the background music.
- The voice sounds helpful and assured, not overly dramatic or sales-heavy.
- The final recording lands between 45 and 55 seconds without sounding rushed.
