# Buyer Journey Video

## Goal

Prove that Sellio delivers connected customer journeys instead of disconnected listing templates.

## Target Length

3-4 minutes. Use chapters so viewers can jump between commerce, bookings, and account management.

## Recommended Narrative

### 1. Discover the marketplace — 00:00-00:35

- Open the strongest unified or ecommerce storefront.
- Search or select a category.
- Apply one meaningful filter.
- Open a listing detail page.
- Briefly show responsive behavior.

**Narration point:** Customers can discover different listing types through consistent, focused storefront experiences.

### 2. Review a listing — 00:35-01:05

- Show gallery, description, attributes, seller/provider information, and related listings.
- Save the listing to favorites if the interaction is stable.
- Avoid scrolling every section; select only the strongest evidence.

### 3. Complete a transaction — 01:05-01:55

Use one primary flow and one brief secondary flow.

**Primary recommendation: product purchase**

- Add an item to cart.
- Open `/cart`.
- Continue to `/checkout`.
- Show the payment experience without exposing keys or entering real card data.
- Cut to a pre-created confirmation page if necessary.

**Secondary montage: property or event booking**

- Choose dates or tickets.
- Show price calculation.
- Show the booking/payment step and confirmation.

### 4. Buyer portal — 01:55-03:05

Record these routes where populated:

- `/` — dashboard overview
- `/favorites` — saved listings
- `/bookings` — booking history
- `/messages` — conversations
- `/applications` — job applications
- `/auto-inquiries` — vehicle inquiries
- `/appointments` and `/quotes` — service activity
- `/classifieds-activity` — classifieds interactions
- `/reviews` — review activity
- `/settings` — account preferences

Do not show every route at full length. Use a 2-3 second montage for secondary vertical activity.

### 5. Close — 03:05-03:25

**Voice-over**

> From first discovery to purchase, booking, inquiry, and ongoing communication, Sellio gives marketplace customers one connected experience.

**Call to action**

`Explore the buyer demo • Available on CodeCanyon`

## Capture Requirements

- Seed a buyer account with favorites, messages, bookings, applications, inquiries, and reviews.
- Use realistic names, totals, dates, and status labels.
- Ensure all thumbnail images load before recording.
- Keep at least one successful confirmation available in a separate tab.
- Hide credentials during the public edit unless they are intentionally promoted as demo credentials.
